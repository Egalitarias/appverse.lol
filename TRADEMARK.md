# Appverse trademark policy

"Appverse", the Appverse logo and the domain appverse.lol (together, the **Appverse marks**) belong to [OWNER].

The software in this repository is licensed under the terms in [LICENSE](LICENSE). That licence covers the code. **It does not give you any right to
use the Appverse marks.** This policy says what you may and may not do with them.

## You may, without asking

- Say that your app, tool or service **works with** or **is compatible with** Appverse, for example "runs on Appverse" or "an Appverse app".
  Use the name in plain text, not as a logo, and make clear you are a separate project.
- Refer to Appverse accurately when you write about it: reviews, tutorials, articles, talks, links to https://appverse.lol.
- Distribute the **unmodified** software, the npm package `appverse` and the downloads from https://appverse.lol, under the name they come with.
- Name your own app anything that is not confusingly similar to the Appverse marks.
- Use the `.applet` file extension. It is a file format name, not a mark we claim.

## You may not, without written permission

- Use "Appverse", or anything confusingly similar (Appverse Pro, Appverse Cloud, Appverze, App-verse, appverse-hosting.com), as the name of a **fork, a
  modified version, a hosted service, a store, a company, a product, a package or a domain name**.
- Use the Appverse logo, or a look that could be mistaken for it, for anything.
- Suggest that your project is made, endorsed, certified or supported by Appverse, or that it is the official one, when it is not.
- Register the Appverse marks, or a confusingly similar name, as a trademark, company name, app-store listing, social-media handle or domain.
- Describe a modified version as "Appverse" without saying that it is modified.

## Forks and modified versions

You are free to fork the code under the licence. If you distribute a modified version or run it as a service, give it **your own name** and remove the
Appverse name and logo, except where you are describing the origin accurately ("based on Appverse"). If you are unsure whether a change counts as modified,
ask.

## Apps people make

An `.applet` that someone writes is theirs, and so is its name. This policy does not restrict naming an app. Appverse does not claim any rights in apps
that people build.

## Asking permission, and reporting misuse

For permission, or to report use you think breaks this policy, write to [CONTACT EMAIL]. Say what you want to do and where the name will appear.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Uses that were fine under an earlier version are fine until you are told otherwise in writing, unless they mislead people.
