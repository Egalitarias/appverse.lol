Privacy

Packages are read and unpacked in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. Apps run with no network access.

Server log and totals

Like any web server, the one behind appverse.lol writes each request to an access log: the time, the page or file asked for, your IP address and your browser's name and language. That log is deleted after 14 days. Once an hour the log is added up into daily totals (page views, visitors, downloads, the site you came from, screen size and language), and only those totals are kept. A visitor is counted from a scrambled mix of address and browser that is thrown away after each run. The totals are shown to the person who runs Appverse, behind a password. There is no tracking script, no cookie and no third party.
